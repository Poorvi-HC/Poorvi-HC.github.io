Publications
Integrated Cross-Organ Transcriptomic Analysis Uncovers Conserved Gene Signatures Predictive of Allograft Rejection
HC Poorvi, PK Vinod
(Preprint) BMC Bioinformatics, 2025
bioRxiv

This research challenges the traditional organ-specific paradigm of transplant rejection by establishing a unified molecular framework across liver, kidney, and heart allografts. By constructing organ-specific gene co-expression networks from 672 biopsies, we identified a conserved six-module immune cascade and a novel 24-gene cell cycle signature, revealing cellular proliferation as a core, previously underappreciated hallmark of rejection pathology. We then distilled these insights into a robust 20-gene universal biomarker panel that predicts rejection with high accuracy (AUC > 0.96).

HyperCLSA: Hypergraph Contrastive Learning with Self-Attention for Multi-Omics Integration
Gaurav Bhole, HC Poorvi, JR Madhav, Prabhakar Bhimalapuram, PK Vinod
(Oral Presentation) 11th International Conference on Pattern Recognition and Machine Intelligence, 2025
bioRxiv

HyperCLSA is a novel deep learning framework designed for robust multi-omics integration in breast cancer subtyping. By synergizing hypergraph convolutional networks, supervised contrastive learning, and adaptive self-attention, the model effectively aligns heterogeneous omics data (mRNA, Methylation, miRNA) to capture high-order biological correlations. Evaluated on the TCGA-BRCA dataset, HyperCLSA achieved state-of-the-art accuracy (90.1%) for PAM50 classification, significantly outperforming established graph-based baselines while maintaining high computational efficiency.
